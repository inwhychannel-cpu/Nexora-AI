GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can Nexora control my laptop right now?+

No. This is a static website and voice-input demo. It can show recognized speech, but it does not execute computer commands. Secure computer control would require a separately installed application with clear permissions and user approval.

Does the microphone listen all the time?+

No. The demo only requests microphone access after you press “Start speaking.” You can stop listening with the same button or revoke permission in your browser settings.

Does it support every language?+

No website can guarantee every language through browser speech recognition. The selector lists sample options, but actual availability depends on your browser, device, and speech-recognition provider.

Where can I try the voice demo?+

Use a modern browser that supports speech recognition and open the site over HTTPS. Some browsers may process speech through their own service; check your browser’s privacy information for details.
